Utilized in ways necessary for policy research and experimentation.

Korea + AI

Connecting Korean AI to the practical capabilities of policy development.

Aiming to create leading cases of AI-based policy development in Korea by applying and publicly verifying domestic AI ecosystem achievements, including independent foundation models, to policy research and development processes.

Utilizing Domestic Models

Comparing utilization characteristics of domestic models such as EXAONE, Solar, and HCX for policy development.

Korean Policy Context

Researching evidence, alternatives, and review procedures within the Korean legal, institutional, and social context.

Open Source Sharing

Sharing methods, code, cases, and failures to enable reuse by future research.
