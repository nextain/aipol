How to use

Connecting AI to each stage of policy development.

You can combine necessary steps and tools suited to your purpose, such as online research, expert workshops, citizen participation, and field experiments.

Problem Definition

Define scope, objectives, and policy levers.

Evidence Research

Structure sources and uncertainties.

Alternative Development

Experts and AI expand options.

Alternative Review

Examine evidence, stakeholder conditions, and conflicting perspectives.

Human Judgment

Revise, select, and approve.

Record and Share

Document the process, outcomes, and limitations.

Ways to apply

Utilize according to research objectives.

Not all cases require events or voting.

Policy Research

Structure literature and evidence to develop policy alternatives and review questions.

Online Collaboration

Asynchronously collect and compare opinions from experts and stakeholders across different times and locations.

Workshops and Deliberation

Utilize AI to organize issues and additional alternatives during expert meetings or citizen deliberation.

Policy Experiment

Verify result differences and failure conditions by varying models, prompts, and procedures.
