Platform

A tool for conducting and sharing policy development research.

Provides researchers with a policy development engine and workspace, and participants with an opinion interface usable online and on-site.

Implemented01 / RESEARCH

Policy Development Engine

Separates policy domains and common procedures, representing levers, evidence, review procedures, protocols, and reproducibility records as code.

  • Domain Plugins
  • Structuring Evidence and Assumptions
  • Protocol Verification
  • Model & Prompt Records
MVP02 / WORKSPACE

Research & Operations Workspace

Creates policy projects, managing alternatives, opinion gathering, AI analysis, human review, and reports step-by-step.

  • Policy Alternative Input & Version Management
  • Online & Field Collection Control
  • AI Task Progress & Retry
  • Human Edit and Approval Gate
MVP03 / PARTICIPATION

Online Participation Interface

Submit positions, conditions, concerns, and reasons regarding policy proposals via browser or mobile. The same interface is utilized for both online and on-site engagement.

  • Mobile and Senior Accessibility
  • Conditional Opinion Collection
  • Linking Judgments by Session
  • Deadline, Error, and Completion Notices

One research record

Connects research, participation, and review into a single record.

Starting from policy evidence and alternatives, it tracks online and on-site opinions, AI analysis, and human decisions within the same project record.

Current boundary

Web-based MVPs are distinguished from public online services.

The public portal and pension experiment preview are delivered as static web pages. A separate FastAPI and SQLite research tool can support controlled studies and events, but is not yet an always-on service for real participant data.

Currently Available

Controlled Online and On-site Research

  • Static public portal and pension experiment preview
  • FastAPI + SQLite tool for controlled research
  • Preparation→Collection→AI Analysis→Human Approval→Report
  • Synthetic Participant Engine Verification
  • De-identified or synthetic static report generation
Operational Enhancement

Always-on public online operation

  • Persistent managed database
  • Personal account and role-based authentication
  • Job queue for long-running AI tasks
  • Personal information minimal collection and destruction system
  • Notice and consent for external AI transfer, plus monitoring
